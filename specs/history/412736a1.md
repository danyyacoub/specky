- **Message:** feat(rendering): draw diagrams as glass in the reader's light or dark scheme

Diagrams were previously rendered as fixed light panels that appeared as bright boxes in dark mode. This change makes the viewer's stylesheet re-point the SVG's colors to the site palette and render boxes as translucent glass panes, with a solid fallback for readers who request reduced transparency, while the full-screen tab inherits the same colors. Exports still keep the original flat light colors for print. Rendering, scrubbing, and diagram CSS/JS were also moved out of `html_render.py` into a new `diagram_render.py` to keep the theme file from growing further, with callers updated to import from there.
