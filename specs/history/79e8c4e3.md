# Control when doc-sync commits land: per commit, per merge, or never

- **Date:** 2026-09-24T17:55:20+02:00
- **Author:** dany <dany.yacoub@gmail.com>
- **Message:** feat(cli): add install-git-hook --on commit|merge|none

## What changed

`specky install-git-hook` now takes `--on commit|merge|none`. `commit` (the default) keeps today's behaviour of a doc commit after every commit, `merge` writes them once per local merge or pull, and `none` installs no hooks so docs are left to `specky sync` or CI. Re-running switches modes, and `specky doctor` plus the plugin's commit trigger follow the recorded mode.

## Why

A doc-sync commit after every commit is noise on a busy branch, and teams merging pull requests on the forge want a CI job on the pull request rather than a doc commit on a shared branch.
