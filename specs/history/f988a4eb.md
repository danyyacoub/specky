# Commit f988a4eb

- **Date:** 2026-09-21T19:52:16+02:00

- **Message:** feat(chat): give the Spec Assistant more room and move its controls to the input

This commit reworks the Spec Assistant panel to give answers more space and put controls where the reader is actually looking. Previously the panel shared the row with the nav rail at a fixed 440px, the intent chips (Auto/Explore/Draft spec) lived up top far from the input, and the "Thinking…" status was a small grey header span that was easy to miss. Now opening the assistant collapses the nav rail (with a new titlebar toggle to bring it back, restored on close), the panel opens wider at 560px with a window-relative max-width that keeps the doc column readable, the intent chips move into a composer bar next to Send, and the status becomes an animated line right above the input. The changes touch `html_render.py` — new `icon-sidebar` sprite, a `--rail-width` CSS variable with a `nav-collapsed` body class, restructured panel markup (composer wrap, `chat-thinking` block, `chat-send` button), and a `nav-toggle` button in the titlebar.
