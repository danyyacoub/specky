Memoize provider calls via CachingProvider wrapper (applied in load_provider_from_toml, the only construction path) to stop re-paying for identical prompts during re-runs. Cache key: sha256(model+prompt), bounded at 20MB with oldest-first eviction. New `specky cost` command reports call count, cache hit rate, and character totals per command+model. Every call (hit or miss) records usage row. Disable with `[ai] cache = false`. Lookup and store swallow SQLite errors (cache is optimization, not load-bearing). Update doctor to unwrap provider before reading concrete fields.
